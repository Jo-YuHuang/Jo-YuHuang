## Design and engineering projects

### Intelligent thread-wrapping machine
**Mechanical design · Arduino · Raspberry Pi · Hall-effect sensing · PID control · testing**

Designed and manufactured a high-speed wrapping subsystem for automated production of core–shell energy-harvesting yarns intended for wearable and biomedical sensing research at Imperial College London's Hamlyn Centre.

Integrated mechanical design with Arduino-based closed-loop motor control, Hall-effect speed sensing, PID control, and Raspberry Pi communication. The system operated stably up to **3,000 RPM**.

Experimental validation achieved **0.176% average steady-state error** and a **0.237 s settling time**. Hall-effect speed measurement was validated against a laser tachometer with a maximum error of **0.44%**. Impact-hammer vibration testing and frequency-response analysis identified dominant structural resonances at approximately **193–232 Hz**, well above the **41.7 Hz** maximum operating frequency.
